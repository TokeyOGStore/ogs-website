Privacy Policy & Personal Data Protection Notice

Last Updated: 14 August 2026

This Notice is intended to be read together with our Terms & Conditions and Return & Refund Policy.

2. Personal Data We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with OGS, we may collect information that can identify or relate to you.

A. Contact and Delivery Information — This may include:

  • Full name
  • Telephone or mobile number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • Billing information where applicable

B. Order Information — This may include:

  • Order number
  • Products purchased
  • Product size and colour
  • Order history
  • Delivery information
  • Payment status
  • Return, refund or exchange information
  • Information provided when contacting customer service

C. Website and Technical Information — When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically by the website, Shopify and other technology providers. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Website activity
  • Referral information
  • Cookies and similar technologies

D. Communications — If you contact OGS, we may retain information relating to your communication with us, including enquiries, messages, complaints and customer service records.

3. How We Collect Your Personal Data

We may collect personal data when you:

  • Visit our website
  • Browse our products
  • Place an order
  • Complete the online checkout
  • Contact Customer Service
  • Contact us by email or WhatsApp
  • Submit information through forms
  • Interact with our social media accounts
  • Use our website or its features
  • Communicate with us directly

We may also receive relevant information from service providers involved in fulfilling your order, such as Shopify, payment providers and courier companies, where permitted by applicable law.

4. Why We Use Your Personal Data

We use personal data for purposes reasonably necessary to operate OGS and provide our services.

Order and Payment Processing — We may use your information to:

  • Process your order
  • Confirm your purchase
  • Process or verify payment
  • Prepare and pack your order
  • Arrange delivery
  • Provide tracking information
  • Handle approved returns, refunds or exchanges

Customer Service — We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Verify your order
  • Handle complaints
  • Assist with delivery issues
  • Investigate product issues
  • Process return, refund or exchange requests

Business Operations — We may also use information to:

  • Maintain transaction records
  • Manage inventory
  • Improve our website and services
  • Understand website usage
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or unauthorised activities
  • Maintain website security and functionality

Legal and Regulatory Purposes — We may process personal data where reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Respond to lawful requests from authorities
  • Establish, exercise or defend legal claims
  • Protect our rights and legitimate business interests

Marketing — Where permitted by applicable law, OGS may use your contact information to send marketing communications such as:

  • New product announcements
  • Promotions
  • Special offers
  • Campaign information

You may request to stop receiving marketing communications at any time.

5. Payment Information

Payments for OGS purchases are made through the online payment methods available on our website.

These may include:

  • FPX
  • Credit cards
  • Debit cards
  • Touch 'n Go
  • Supported e-wallets

Payment processing may be handled by third-party payment service providers.

OGS does not need to receive or store your complete card payment credentials where those details are processed directly by the relevant payment provider.

The payment provider may collect and process payment information according to its own privacy policy and security practices.

6. Shopify and Other Service Providers

The OGS website uses Shopify and may use other third-party technology and service providers to operate the online store.

These providers may process information for purposes including:

  • Website operation
  • Shopping cart functionality
  • Checkout
  • Payment processing
  • Order management
  • Website analytics
  • Security
  • Customer communications

Other service providers may include payment providers, courier companies, hosting/security providers, analytics services and other technology providers necessary to operate our business.

Where applicable, these providers may process or store information outside Malaysia.

OGS will take reasonable steps to ensure personal data is handled in accordance with applicable Malaysian data protection requirements.

7. Disclosure of Personal Data

OGS does not sell your personal data.

Where reasonably necessary, we may disclose or provide access to relevant personal data to selected third parties, including:

  • Shopify and e-commerce service providers
  • Payment service providers
  • Ninja Van and J&T Express
  • IT and technology service providers
  • Website/security providers
  • Professional advisers where necessary
  • Government authorities or law enforcement agencies where required or permitted by law

We aim to provide only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies.

These technologies may help us:

  • Keep the website functioning properly
  • Maintain shopping cart functionality
  • Remember certain preferences
  • Understand website usage
  • Improve website performance
  • Support analytics
  • Support marketing activities where applicable

You may manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

9. Personal Data Security

OGS takes reasonable steps to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, misuse, loss, destruction or alteration.

We may use appropriate technical and organisational measures depending on the nature of the information and the services involved.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Retention of Personal Data

OGS will retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including our business, accounting, legal and regulatory requirements.

When personal data is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, destroy or anonymise it where appropriate.

The actual retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is retained.

11. Accuracy of Your Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and up-to-date information.

This is particularly important when providing:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • Order information

If you believe that information held by OGS is inaccurate or incomplete, please contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, update the information.

12. Your Personal Data Rights

Subject to applicable law and any applicable exceptions, you may have rights relating to your personal data, including rights to:

  • Request access to personal data held by OGS
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Make enquiries about how your personal data is processed
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Request information regarding the processing of your personal data

Certain requests may require identity verification and may be subject to applicable legal requirements, limitations or fees.

The Personal Data Protection Act provides data subjects with rights relating to access and correction of personal data.

13. Withdrawing Consent

Where OGS processes your personal data based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent by contacting us.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Please note that OGS may still need to retain or process certain information where permitted or required by law or where necessary to fulfil an existing transaction or other legitimate purpose.

14. Marketing Communications

If you have agreed to receive marketing communications from OGS, you may request to stop receiving them at any time.

You can contact our Customer Service team to make such a request.

Stopping marketing communications will not prevent us from sending essential communications relating to:

  • Existing orders
  • Payments
  • Delivery
  • Returns/refunds
  • Customer service matters

15. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general consumers and is not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children for purposes that are not permitted by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal data to OGS without appropriate authorisation, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

16. Transfer of Personal Data

Some of our technology and service providers may operate outside Malaysia.

Where personal data is transferred outside Malaysia, OGS will take reasonable steps to ensure that the transfer and subsequent processing are handled in accordance with applicable Malaysian data protection requirements.

The Personal Data Protection framework has been amended in 2024, with commencement dates for different provisions occurring in stages during 2025.

17. Personal Data Breaches

If OGS becomes aware of a personal data breach that requires notification under applicable law or regulatory requirements, we will take appropriate steps to investigate and address the incident and make any required notifications.

18. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website or social media channels may contain links to third-party websites or services.

These third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms.

OGS is not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites.

We encourage customers to review the privacy policies of third-party websites before providing personal information to them.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

OGS may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in:

  • Our business operations
  • Website functionality
  • Technology
  • Services
  • Legal or regulatory requirements
  • Data protection practices

The latest version will be published on the OGS website together with the relevant Last Updated date.
