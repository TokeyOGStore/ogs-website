FAQ

Before You Continue.

Original Garment Store (OGS) is a men's apparel destination bringing together quality menswear from multiple brands in one place.

OGS focuses on menswear across eight categories: Jeans, Chino, Cargo, Carpenter, Utilities, Shirt, T-Shirt and Jacket — from a range of selected brands.

Every man has different tastes and needs. By bringing multiple brands together in one place, we want to give you more choice without having to search across different stores.

This website is not the checkout. It's built to introduce OGS and our collections. The actual purchase — product selection, cart and checkout — happens on the official OGS store on Shopify.

You'll be taken straight to shop.originalgarmentstore.com.my, the official OGS store on Shopify, where you can view the full product range, choose variants (size/colour) and check out securely.

Delivery is handled by Ninja Van and J&T at a flat RM5.50 fee (subject to change by the courier from time to time) — we ship across Malaysia, including Sabah and Sarawak. Estimated delivery: 1–2 days for Peninsular Malaysia, 3–5 days for Labuan, Sabah and Sarawak. Tracking numbers are sent by email and can be checked on the Shopify website. Orders placed on working days are processed the same day or the next working day; weekend/public holiday orders are processed the following working day. There's no minimum order amount. Payment is only through the website — we accept FPX, Credit Card, Debit Card, Touch 'n Go and e-wallets.

Generally, purchased items are not eligible for exchange or return due to wrong size, change of mind or personal preference. An exchange is only considered if the error is confirmed to be on OGS's part (e.g. wrong product/size sent, or a defect present before shipping) — requests must be made as soon as possible within 7 days of receiving the item, no processing fee is charged, and approved refunds are processed within 7 working days. OGS does not offer a product warranty. Read our full Return & Refund Policy.
